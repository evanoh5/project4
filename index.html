ABOUT

Hey! Thanks for stopping by. I'm a lifelong learner and spend most of my time either working with amazing people or am on a mission to pursue my next project that's in a domain I haven't touched before. Much of this spirit is inspired by the work of Aaron Sorkin and the West Wing, which also inspired me to name my contact email blockofcheeseday@gmail.com. I'm always looking for good conversation so let me know if I can be helpful or if you'd like to chat! As previewed, I can be reached at blockofcheeseday@gmail.com.
